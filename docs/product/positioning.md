# LUVOY — Positioning

## Brand Positioning

LUVOY is positioned as:
- luxury-tech travel platform
- premium curated travel experience
- editorial-inspired travel app

The product combines:
- luxury hospitality
- editorial storytelling
- technology
- intelligent curation

---

## Competitors

Indirect competitors:
- Airbnb Luxe
- Booking Premium
- Wanderlog
- TripAdvisor
- Google Travel

LUVOY differentiates through:
- premium visual identity
- emotional UX
- editorial storytelling
- luxury-inspired aesthetics
- curated experiences

---

## Brand Personality

LUVOY must feel:
- calm
- elegant
- aspirational
- intelligent
- premium
- modern

Avoid:
- flashy luxury
- excessive promotions
- noisy interfaces
- overcrowded layouts
