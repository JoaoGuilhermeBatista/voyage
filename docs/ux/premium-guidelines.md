# LUVOY — Premium Guidelines

## Core UX Philosophy

Luxury is simplicity.

The app must:
- breathe
- feel editorial
- prioritize imagery
- prioritize whitespace
- avoid clutter

---

## Visual Density

Always prefer:
- less information
- more spacing
- visual calm
- larger imagery

Avoid:
- dashboard feeling
- crowded cards
- excessive labels
- multiple CTAs

---

## Imagery

Photography is part of navigation.

Preferred imagery:
- mediterranean aesthetics
- warm sunlight
- luxury resorts
- boutique hotels
- elegant architecture
- calm compositions

---

## UI Feel

The app should feel like:
- luxury magazine
- boutique hotel brand
- premium concierge

Never feel like:
- e-commerce
- generic SaaS
- booking marketplace
