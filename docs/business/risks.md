# LUVOY — Risks

## Product Risks

### Scope Creep
Risk:
Adding too many features during MVP.

Mitigation:
Strict MVP definition.

---

### Generic UX
Risk:
Losing premium perception.

Mitigation:
Strict design system.

---

## Technical Risks

### Google Maps Cost
Mitigation:
Caching and request optimization.

---

### Vendor Lock-in
Mitigation:
Modular integrations.

---

### Scalability
Mitigation:
Feature-first architecture.

---

## Business Risks

### Churn
Mitigation:
Strong onboarding and premium retention.

---

### Competition
Mitigation:
Luxury positioning and emotional UX.
